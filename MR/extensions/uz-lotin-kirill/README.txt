Uz Lotin ↔ Kirill — Chrome extension
====================================

O‘rnatish
1. chrome://extensions oching
2. Yuqorida "Developer mode" ni yoqing
3. "Load unpacked" bosing
4. Shu papkani tanlang: uz-lotin-kirill

Qanday ishlaydi
- Ikonkani bosing: 1 ta inputga matn yozing, natija pastda chiqadi, "Nusxalash" bilan clipboardga oling.
- Sahifada matnni belgilang va Ctrl+Shift bosing — o‘girilgan matn clipboardga tushadi.
- O‘rnatgach ochiq tabni yangilang. chrome:// sahifalarda ishlamaydi.
- Matn ustida o‘ng tugma: "Lotin ↔ Kirill (clipboard)"

Sozlamalar (3 rejim)
- Replace (default): lotin harflarini kirillga, kirillni lotinga almashtiradi. Aralash matnda ham ishlaydi.
- Lotin → Kirill: hammasi kirillga
- Kirill → Lotin: hammasi lotinga

Shortcutni o‘zgartirish: chrome://extensions/shortcuts
